## 1. Institutional Mandate
The **Praxeophysics** theoretical framework and the **Praxeophysics Protocol** specifications published on `praxeophysics.org` are developed and maintained by the **Praxeophysics Institute** as a strictly domain-neutral, open academic and technical commons.

3. **Royalty-Free Interoperability**: The Praxeophysics Institute asserts no royalty claims or restrictive licensing barriers against individuals or organizations implementing compatible systems that conform to these open specifications.

2. **Strict Separation of Core Standard vs. External Application Adapters**: In accordance with [[Claim_Attribute_Rule_Separation|Structural Separation of Personal Attributes and Contextual Rules]] ([[Term_Attribute_Rule_Separation|Attribute-Rule Separation]]), the open standard governed by the Praxeophysics Institute covers strictly the domain-neutral upper-layer architecture:

   * [[Term_Verification|Verification]] pipelines ([[Term_Selective_Disclosure|Selective Disclosure]], [[Term_ZKP|Zero-Knowledge Proofs]], and [[Term_Plausible_Deniability|Plausible Deniability]]).
