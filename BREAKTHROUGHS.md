## 1. Trade-off 1 (Insulation vs. Coupling): Markov Blankets, Interior Opacity, and Fiduciary AI
* **Pathologies**: Pole 1A treats agents as static types ([[Term_Svabhava|Svabhāva]]) independent of temporal history, whereas Pole 1B dissolves individual statistical boundaries into surveillance networks and conflates non-living AI models with biological principals.
* **Praxeophysics Resolution ([[Claim_Statistical_Boundary|Statistical Boundary and Individuated Existence]], [[Claim_Interior_Opacity_Principle|Principle of Interior Opacity and Apoha Boundary Sufficiency]], [[Claim_Extended_Blanket_Fiduciary_Agency|Property Boundaries and Fiduciary AI as Extended Markov Blankets]])**: Enforces conditional independence ($\eta \perp \mu \mid b$) via the **Principle of Interior Opacity**: raw $(\boldsymbol{\mu}, \boldsymbol{\Pi}, L(t))$ and the tacit margin $\ker\Phi$ never leave the [[Term_Storage_Holder|Personal Data Store]]. Only negative [[Term_Boundary_Envelope|Boundary Envelopes]] $[a_d(t), b_d(t)]$ are exported. Furthermore, AI agents—lacking intrinsic biological [[Term_NESS|NESS]]—are bound strictly as [[Term_Extended_Markov_Blanket|Extended Markov Blankets]] under [[Term_Fiduciary_Duty|Fiduciary Duty]] ([[Ref_Friston_2019|Friston (2019)]], [[Ref_Parr_2020|Parr, Da Costa, & Friston (2020)]], [[Ref_Ashby_1956|Ashby (1956)]], [[Ref_Coase_1960|Coase (1960)]]).

## 2. Trade-off 2 (Complexity vs. Accuracy): Two-Truths Separation and Orthogonal Guardrails
* **Pathologies**: In $F = \text{Complexity} - \text{Accuracy}$, maximizing accuracy alone leads to Pole 2A (behavioral over-specification crowding out embodied [[Term_Tacit_Knowledge|Tacit Knowledge]]), whereas excessively penalizing complexity leads to Pole 2B (collapsing multi-dimensional states into a 1D scalar score).
* **Praxeophysics Resolution ([[Claim_Two_Truths_Tacit_Separation|Two-Truths Separation of Continuous Tacit Reality and Discrete Guardrails]], [[Claim_Rejection_Of_Scalar_Reduction|Rejection of Single-Scalar Reduction and Preservation of Orthogonality]], [[Claim_Dimensionless_Normalized_Space|Projection onto a Dimensionless Normalized State Space and Precision Commensurability]])**: Synthesizes [[Term_Apoha|Apoha]] exclusion with [[Person_Michael_Polanyi|Polanyi]]'s tacit dimension: discrete [[Term_Atomic_Predicate|Atomic Predicates]] $x_d(t) \in \{0, 1\}^D$ act strictly as negative outer guardrails over a [[Term_Dimensionless_Normalization|Dimensionless Normalized Feature Space]], leaving the viable interior $\operatorname{int}(\mathcal{U}_i)$ completely unregimented ([[Ref_Polanyi_1966|Polanyi (1966)]], [[Ref_Tillemans_2021|Tillemans (2021)]], [[Ref_Simon_1962|Simon (1962)]]).

## 3. Trade-off 3 (Sensory vs. Prior Precision): Allostatic Load Relief and Attribute–Rule Separation
* **Pathologies**: Unbuffered sensory error ($\Pi_{\text{sensory}} \gg \Pi_{\text{prior}}$) drives chronic [[Term_Allostatic_Load|Normalized Allostatic Load]] $L(t) \to 1$ (Pole 3A), whereas prior hyper-precision ($\Pi_{\text{prior}} \to \infty$) reifies external standards ([[Term_Prapanca|Conceptual Reification]]) and locks user attributes $\mathbf{x}$ into platform silos $R_c$ (Pole 3B).
* **Praxeophysics Resolution ([[Claim_Uniform_Standard_Allostatic_Load|Over-Adaptation to Uniform Standards and Accumulation of Allostatic Load]], [[Claim_Emptiness_Precision_Regularization|De-reification via Emptiness and Higher-Order Precision Regularization]], [[Claim_Attribute_Rule_Separation|Structural Separation of Personal Attributes and Contextual Rules]])**: Derives narrow protective radii $r_d(t) = \sqrt{2(1-L(t))/\Pi_d}$ from positive precision commitments ([[Term_Inverse_Precision_Inference|Inverse Precision Inference]]), suspends alignment demands via `ALLOSTATIC_LOAD_RELIEF` when $L(t) \ge \theta_L$, and decouples personal attributes from contextual rule circuits ($\mathbf{x} \perp R_c$) ([[Ref_McEwen_1998|McEwen (1998)]], [[Ref_Deane_2020|Deane, Miller, & Wilkinson (2020)]], [[Ref_Laukkonen_2025|Laukkonen, Friston, & Chandaria (2025)]], [[Ref_Westerhoff_2024|Westerhoff (2024)]]).

## 4. Trade-off 4 (Exploitation vs. Exploration): Resolving the Dark-Room Paradox via EFE
* **Pathologies**: Pushing exploration under depleted capacity $1-L(t)$ causes boundary breakdown (Pole 4A), whereas penalizing exploration variance $\sigma_{\pi,d}^2$ inside the viable zone via $\frac{1}{2}\Pi_d \sigma_{\pi,d}^2 > \frac{1}{2}\ln(1+\Pi_d\sigma_{\pi,d}^2)$ traps the agent in the Dark Room ($\sigma_{\pi,d}^2 \to 0$, Pole 4B).
* **Praxeophysics Resolution ([[Claim_Action_Axiom_EFE_Duality|Duality of Expected Free Energy Minimization and the Action Axiom]], [[Claim_Pragmatic_Epistemic_Decomposition|Decomposition into Pragmatic Value and Epistemic Value]], [[Claim_Homeostatic_Capacity_Unlocks_Exploration|Unlocking Epistemic Exploration via Homeostatic Capacity Expansion]])**: `evaluateExpectedFreeEnergy` enforces boundary-envelope inclusion $\lvert \hat{s}_{\pi,d} - \mu_d \rvert + \sigma_{\pi,d} \le r_d(t)$ while leaving interior exploration variance unpenalized, so lowering $L(t)$ expands $r_d(t)$ and directly unlocks Shannon–KL [[Term_Epistemic_Value|Epistemic Value]] ([[Ref_Friston_2012|Friston, Thornton, & Clark (2012)]], [[Ref_Friston_2019|Friston (2019)]], [[Ref_Mises_1949|Mises (1949/1998)]]).
